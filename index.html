Chapter 01

My Story

Why I chose Computer Science, how I got started, and how I think about building things.

Why Computer Science?

I've always been drawn to problems that have a clear, logical answer if you look hard enough. CS felt like the one field where I could keep chasing that feeling for the rest of my career.

How I started

My first year at FAST-NUCES threw me straight into C++ and Object-Oriented Programming. It was overwhelming at first, but writing my first working program made it click.

What motivates me

I like building things I can actually see work — a game, a calculator, a tool a friend can use. Watching something go from a blank file to a working program never gets old.

How I think

I break problems into small pieces before writing a single line of code. It's slower at first, but it saves me hours of confused debugging later.
